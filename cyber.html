MODULE 01

CYBERSECURITY BASICS

Understand the fundamental ideas behind cybersecurity before moving into networking and advanced security topics.

01

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and data from unauthorized access and attacks.

02

Confidentiality

Confidentiality means information should only be accessible to authorized people.

03

Integrity

Integrity means information should remain accurate and should not be changed improperly.

04

Availability

Availability means systems and information should be accessible when authorized users need them.

05

Threat

A threat is something that can potentially cause harm to a system or its information.

06

Vulnerability

A vulnerability is a weakness that could potentially be exploited.
